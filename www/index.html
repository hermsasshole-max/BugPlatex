General June Beetle

Phyllophaga spp.

Green-Brown Fluids Chunky Cruncher

"A brave, chunky aviator whose armored carapace met 70 MPH minivan aerodynamics."

🧠 Road-Trip Bug Clues
  • 1 June bugs are notoriously terrible pilots and fly like tiny blimps with no steering wheel.
  • 2 Their thick exoskeletons are made of chitin, the same tough material in lobster and crab claws!
  • 3 Most June bugs spend 2 to 3 years underground as grubs before flying for just a few weeks.
🧽
Clean: Soak the dried chitin with warm soapy water for 3 minutes before wiping to prevent windshield scratches!
❤️

Lovebug Road-Trip Duo

Plecia nearctica

Red-Black Chitin Winged Flyer

"United in life and united on the windshield glass forever."

🧠 Road-Trip Bug Clues
  • 1 Lovebugs fly in glued-together pairs during May and September across southern highways.
  • 2 Their body fluids are mildly acidic and will bake into automotive clear coat under hot sunshine!
  • 3 They are attracted to hot highway asphalt, engine fumes, and the smell of decomposing grass.
🧽
Clean: Clean immediately with a wet dryer sheet or baking soda paste — never let lovebug juice bake in the sun!
🐝

Golden Honeybee

Apis mellifera

Yellow Pollen Gloop Sticky Pollen

"A sweet pollen collector on her final highway nectar patrol. Rest in wildflower peace."

🧠 Road-Trip Bug Clues
  • 1 The yellow splatter stain is actually pure floral pollen harvested by the bee's hairy hind legs!
  • 2 A worker honeybee visits over 2,000 flowers in a single busy summer day.
  • 3 Honeybees flap their wings 200 times every second, creating their famous buzzing sound.
🧽
Clean: Yellow pollen residue dissolves fast with a 50/50 mix of white vinegar and water.
🦋

Velvet Highway Moth

Lymantria dispar

Dusty Grey Scales Winged Flyer

"It followed the brightest light on Interstate 80 all the way to the glass."

🧠 Road-Trip Bug Clues
  • 1 The chalky grey smudge comes from thousands of microscopic powdery scales covering their wings.
  • 2 Moths navigate using the light of the moon, which is why headlights completely confuse them!
  • 3 Some moths have fake owl-eyes painted on their wings to scare off bats and birds.
🧽
Clean: Wipe with a dry microfiber cloth first to remove dusty scales, then spray windshield washer fluid.

Electric Blue Dragonfly

Pachydiplax longipennis

Electric Blue Sheen Winged Flyer

"The undisputed stunt pilot of the pond, outmaneuvered only by tempered glass."

🧠 Road-Trip Bug Clues
  • 1 Dragonflies can fly backwards, upside down, and hover completely still in mid-air!
  • 2 They have 30,000 lenses in each eye, giving them nearly 360-degree vision around them.
  • 3 Dragonflies are among the most successful predators in nature, catching 95% of their targeted prey.
🧽
Clean: The thin translucent fluid clears easily with regular wiper fluid and fresh wiper blades.

Dog-Day Screaming Cicada

Green-Brown Fluids Chunky Cruncher

"Spent 17 quiet years underground only to meet our SUV at mile marker 42."

🚗 Rule #1: Road-Trip Detective Safety

Safety is our top highway priority! The driver focuses solely on the road. The co-pilot or kids in the back seats are the official SplatDex Splatter Investigators. Never snap pictures from the driver seat while moving.

📸 Pro Photography Tips for Splatter Detection

  • • The Paper Trick: Hold a white notebook or sheet of paper inside the car 2 inches behind the splatter. This blocks sky glare and illuminates fluid colorimetry!
  • • Focus on the Glass: Tap your phone screen directly on the bug residue so the lens locks focus on the glass instead of the distant road.
  • • Angle the Light: Side daylight highlights chitin reflections and wing fragments.

🔬 Splatter Anatomy & Fluid Decoder

🟡 Yellow Fluid Pollen collectors (Bees, Wasps). Sugar-rich and sticky residue.
🟢 Green-Brown Herbivores (Grasshoppers, Cicadas). Digested plant chlorophyll.
🔴 Red / Crimson Predators / Blood-feeders (Mosquitoes, Ladybugs). Micro droplets.
⚪ Dusty Grey Lepidoptera (Moths, Mayflies). Powdery chitin scales.

⚠️ The Golden Rule of Windscreen Care

Never scrape dry bug splatters with credit cards, keys, or dry wipers! Insect exoskeletons contain hard chitin that can scratch automotive glass and ruin wiper rubber. Always soften them first.

✨ Hack #1: The Dryer Sheet Miracle

Take an ordinary laundry dryer sheet, wet it with water, and gently rub over baked-on bugs. The fabric softener agents instantly dissolve insect proteins without scratching glass or car clearcoat!

🧪 Hack #2: 50/50 White Vinegar Spray

Mix equal parts white vinegar and warm water in a spray bottle. Vinegar neutralizes acidic lovebug fluids and dissolves sticky honeybee pollen in 60 seconds flat.

⏱️ Lovebug 48-Hour Warning

Lovebug bodies turn acidic as they decompose under hot highway sun. If left on paint for more than 48 hours, the acid can etch your car clearcoat. Wash off at the next gas station!
